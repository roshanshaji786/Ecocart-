# EcoCart Relaunch Plan — ecocart.co.in

*Prepared 15 Oct 2026. Diwali is Sun 8 Nov 2026 (Dhanteras Fri 6 Nov), so you have about 3 weeks of peak gifting season left. That is why the relaunch has to happen in 2 days, not 2 months.*

> **How this was researched:** the live site could not be opened from my research environment, so this audit is based on the pages of ecocart.co.in that search engines have indexed (some are 1–2 years old), plus competitor and market research. Before Day 1, open your site and confirm each problem below still exists. Sources are listed at the end.

---

## 1. The short version

1. **The platform is not the main reason orders are low.** The bigger reasons are: no clear identity ("one-stop eco shop" is what Brown Living and Beco already own), a catalogue that mixes unrelated things, template leftovers that hurt trust, and very little traffic.
2. **The fix is focus.** Relaunch as **"Kerala's plastic-free gift & home store — handmade in Kerala, delivered across India."** Your Thiruvananthapuram base is the one thing the big eco brands cannot copy.
3. **Move to Shopify** for a faster, cleaner store with Indian payment, COD and shipping tools. Start with about 25–30 strong products, not all 94.
4. **Launch straight into Diwali and corporate gifting**: gift hampers at ₹999 / ₹1,499 / ₹2,499 plus custom logo hampers for companies (Technopark and Infopark are right next to you).
5. **Traffic is the real work after launch.** Orders = visitors × conversion rate. A new store with no visitors still gets no orders. Section 7 covers the first 30 days.

## 2. Audit: what is holding ecocart.co.in back

### 2.1 No clear identity
- The shop lists ~94 products across eco goods, grains & flours, honey, sprouted-moong chips, coconut shell cups, virgin coconut oil, bamboo tissue, handmade soaps, **men's thobes, prayer mats, dresses, diaper hampers, customised wallets, flower bouquets** and even a **"Smartphone"** category.
- A first-time visitor cannot answer "what is EcoCart?" within 5 seconds, so they leave.
- The headline promise is *"Eco-Friendly Products: Your One-Stop Shop in India."* That is exactly the position of **Brown Living** (a marketplace of verified sustainable brands) and **Beco** (coconut + bamboo home care, thousands of reviews). You cannot out-range them. You can out-*story* them.

### 2.2 Template leftovers that kill trust
| Found on the site | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| "Free Shipping on All Orders Over **$100**" | Dollar sign on an Indian store looks like a copied template |
| Three different shipping claims: "Free shipping on all order", "free shipping within Kerala", "complimentary shipping on most orders" | Customer doesn't know what they will pay; surprises at checkout cause cart abandonment |
| A "Home 2" page and a "Smartphone" category | Leftovers from the theme demo |
| Two different phone numbers, plus "free from fixed and mobile phones in India" text | Generic template text; one clear WhatsApp number builds more trust |
| "Almost sold out" on the chips page | If it isn't true, it is false urgency. Shoppers notice it, and India's dark-pattern guidelines (CCPA, 2023) prohibit it |

### 2.3 SEO problems (why Google doesn't send you buyers)
| Current product name / URL | Problem | Better |
|---|---|---|
| `𝕋𝕙𝕠𝕓𝕖_ℕ𝕒𝕧𝕪_𝔹𝕝𝕦𝕖`, `𝕊𝔼𝕐 - White`, `Saffron-Infused-Ho𝕟𝕖𝕪` | Fancy Unicode letters are *different characters*. Nobody searches for them, Google can't match them, screen readers read them as maths symbols | `Men's Cotton Thobe – Navy Blue`, `Saffron-Infused Honey – 250 g` |
| Bamboo tissue at `/product/product/` | URL says nothing | `/products/bamboo-facial-tissues` |
| "Discover the Delight of Homemade Natural Soaps: Unique Flavors and Shapes" | Blog headline used as a product name | `Handmade Natural Soap – [Scent] – 100 g` |
| "Benefits of Virgin Coconut Oil by Meswa Naturals" | Same | `Cold-Pressed Virgin Coconut Oil – 200 ml` |
| "Chips : A Step Towards Sustainable Snacking" | Same | `Sprouted Moong Chips – 250 g` |

### 2.4 Content that doesn't sell
- Only 4 blog posts since May 2024. Two are off-topic: a news piece on the Gaza conflict ("All Eyes on Rafah") and a founder profile ("Roshan Shaji: Kerala's AI Visionary and Entrepreneur").
  - Remove news and political posts from a shop blog. They confuse shoppers and search engines about what the site is for.
  - The founder profile is useful material. **Turn it into the "Our Story" page**, focused on why you started EcoCart in Thiruvananthapuram.
- No visible customer reviews were found in the index.

### 2.5 Reselling other brands with no reason to buy from you
- Products like Meswa Naturals coconut oil, Pure Pack chips and GreenNuts desiccated coconut can be bought on Amazon, often cheaper. Unless you add something (a curated hamper, a Kerala-sourced story, a gift wrap), there is no reason to choose EcoCart.

### 2.6 Name confusion
- "EcoCart" is also a well-known US company (ecocart.io, a carbon-offset app **for Shopify stores**), an Egyptian bag store (ecocart.co) and a Delhi trading firm. Searching "ecocart" shows the US company first.
- Don't rename in 2 days. Instead, **always pair the name with a descriptor**: logo lock-up "EcoCart · Kerala", and SEO titles like "EcoCart Kerala – Handmade, Plastic-Free Gifts".
- Later, check the trademark position for "EcoCart" in your classes on the IP India public search before investing heavily in the name.

### 2.7 What's already good (keep it)
- A real local base in Thiruvananthapuram and real contacts.
- Kerala-native products: coconut shell cups (₹150), virgin coconut oil (₹449–₹1,199), handmade soaps, honey, coconut-based foods.
- You already do **customised wallets and custom gift hampers**. That is the seed of a corporate gifting business.
- A Facebook page called "EcoCart" ("natural, handmade and eco friendly products… All Local made", ~11.5k likes). If it's yours, it is a ready audience for launch day.

## 3. New positioning and USPs

### 3.1 Positioning statement
> **EcoCart is Kerala's plastic-free gift and home store. Every product is handmade or sourced in Kerala, packed without plastic, and gift-ready. We deliver across India.**

### 3.2 Tagline options (pick one)
- **"Handmade in Kerala. Never in plastic."** *(recommended)*
- "Good things from God's Own Country."
- "Zero-waste gifts, straight from Kerala."

### 3.3 Five USPs (put these on the homepage, product pages and every ad)
1. **Made in Kerala, by people with names.** Every product page and every box has an artisan card: photo, name, village, what they make. Trivandrum is a traditional centre of coconut-shell craft and the Balaramapuram handloom cluster is next door. Use that.
2. **100% plastic-free packing, proven.** Paper, cloth, coir, no tape-wrapped plastic. Show it in an unboxing reel. (Only say "100%" once it is actually true for every order.)
3. **Gift-ready by default: the EcoCart cloth wrap.** Every order ships in a reusable handloom cloth wrap (off-white with a gold/kasavu-style border, furoshiki style). The wrap is part of the gift, is easy to recognise in photos, and is hard for competitors to copy. If the cost is too high at first, use a cotton/jute pouch with a kasavu-border ribbon and upgrade later.
4. **Custom & corporate gifting.** Logo-printed wallets, bags and cards. Minimum order 20, sample box available, GST invoice.
5. **Simple, honest promises.** Ships in 24–48 hours · COD across India · Free shipping over ₹999 · 7-day easy returns on damaged or wrong items.

### 3.4 Watch your green claims
Avoid vague claims like "chemical-free", "100% eco" or "planet-positive" unless you can prove them. The CCPA's 2024 guidelines on greenwashing require environmental claims to be specific and backed by evidence. Say exactly what is true instead: "coconut shell, hand-polished, food-safe beeswax finish" or "packed in kraft paper and cotton".

### 3.5 Who we sell to
| Customer | What they want | How we reach them |
|---|---|---|
| **Gift buyers, 25–45, in metro cities** (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai) | A gift that feels thoughtful, not another box of sweets | Instagram reels, Meta ads, Google Shopping |
| **Malayalis living outside Kerala** | A taste of home: coconut oil, honey, Kerala snacks, kasavu | Malayali community groups, WhatsApp, Onam/Vishu/Christmas campaigns |
| **Corporate HR/admin teams** (Technopark, Infopark, Kerala businesses, then metros) | Diwali/Christmas/New Year gifts that look good and are easy to order in bulk | Direct outreach, LinkedIn, a corporate enquiry form |
| **Conscious home buyers** | Plastic-free kitchen and bath swaps | SEO, blog, bundles |

## 4. New catalogue

Launch with **about 25–30 products in 4 collections**. Fewer, better products convert better than 94 mixed ones.

| Collection | What goes in | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Coconut Kitchen** | Coconut shell cups, bowls, spoons; virgin coconut oil; desiccated coconut | Your most "Kerala" line. Hero collection. |
| **Natural Self-Care** | Handmade soaps, coconut oil (body use), bamboo tissue | Sell soaps in sets of 3 |
| **Kerala Pantry** | Honey (incl. saffron-infused), sprouted moong chips, spices, grains in eco packs | Good for hamper fillers |
| **Gift Hampers** (Diwali first, then Christmas/New Year) | Curated boxes in the cloth wrap | **The main money-maker for Oct–Dec** |

**What to do with the rest:**
- Before moving anything, look at the last 12 months of WooCommerce sales. **Keep anything that actually sells**, even if it doesn't fit the theme.
- Thobes, prayer mats, dresses and diaper hampers: if they sell, keep them as a separate, hidden collection you link to directly (or a seasonal Ramadan/Eid collection). Don't show them on the homepage or main menu.
- Delete the "Smartphone" category, "Home 2" and anything that hasn't sold in a year.

### 4.1 Pricing and bundles
- A ₹150 coconut cup alone barely covers shipping. **Sell sets**: "Coconut Cup Set of 2 + 2 spoons" ~₹449. "Soap Trio" instead of single soaps.
- **Free shipping over ₹999** (nationwide). Below that, a flat fee (e.g. ₹79). Optionally free over ₹499 within Kerala.
- **5% off on prepaid/UPI** to cut COD returns.
- Target average order value: **₹1,200+**.
- Check margins on every hamper: product cost + wrap + box + shipping + gateway fee (~2%) + COD loss allowance. Aim for at least 50% gross margin on hampers.

### 4.2 Diwali hamper line-up (build these from what you already stock)

|---|---|---|

**Last order date for Diwali delivery: Sun 1 Nov** (gives 5–7 days for shipping across India). Show a countdown banner.

### 4.3 Product page template
1. **Title:** what it is – key variant – size (e.g. "Coconut Shell Cup Set – 2 Cups + 2 Spoons")
2. **3–5 photos:** plain background, in use (lifestyle), in a hand (for scale), the packaging/wrap
3. **First line:** one-sentence benefit ("Your morning chai, in a cup carved from a coconut that would have been burned.")
4. **Who made it:** artisan name + 1 line
5. **What it's made of / how it's eco:** specific and true
6. **Size, care, ingredients**
7. **Delivery & returns line:** "Ships in 24–48 h · COD available · Free shipping over ₹999"
8. **Reviews**

## 5. Platform: why Shopify, and what it costs

**Recommendation: move to Shopify (Basic plan).** Reasons:
- Hosting, speed, security and updates are handled. No plugin breakage.
- Mobile checkout that customers already trust.
- Indian ecosystem is plug-and-play: Razorpay/Cashfree payments, Shiprocket shipping, COD verification, WhatsApp tools.
- You already know the Shopify admin from your other business.

**Important:** create a **new, separate Shopify store for EcoCart**. Don't add EcoCart to your existing Shopify store; each store has its own plan.

**Approximate costs (check shopify.com/in/pricing; prices below are from 2026 third-party guides):**
| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Shopify Basic | ~₹1,994/month (monthly billing) or ~₹1,499/month (yearly billing), + 18% GST |
| Shopify transaction fee | Shopify Payments isn't available in India, so Basic reportedly adds ~2% on third-party gateways. Verify on the pricing page. |
| Razorpay gateway | ~2% per transaction |
| Shiprocket | Pay per shipment, depending on weight and courier |
| Apps | Start on free tiers |

**Migration approach: a clean start, not a copy.**
- Import only the 25–30 chosen products (CSV or a WooCommerce importer app such as AppsByB WooCommerce Importer, which also brings customers, orders and reviews).
- Import your **customer list** so you can invite past buyers back.
- **Keep the domain ecocart.co.in** and add **301 redirects** from old URLs so Google rankings and old links carry over:
  - `/product/<slug>/` → `/products/<new-handle>`
  - `/shop/` → `/collections/all`
  - `/product-category/<cat>/` → `/collections/<new-collection>`
  - `/blog/` → `/blogs/news`
- **Back up WordPress first** (full files + database + WooCommerce exports). Keep the old hosting for 30 days.
- **Protect your email.** If contact@ecocart.co.in is hosted with your web host, change **only** the A record and `www` CNAME. Do not touch MX records.
  - Shopify DNS: `A @ → 23.227.38.65` and `CNAME www → shops.myshopify.com`. Shopify issues SSL automatically.

## 7. The next 30 days (this is where orders come from)

### Week 1 (18–25 Oct): Diwali push + corporate outreach
- **Corporate:** make a list of 50 companies (start with Technopark Trivandrum and Infopark Kochi). Find HR/admin contacts on LinkedIn. Send a short message + PDF + offer to drop off a free sample box locally. Even 3–5 corporate orders of 20–50 boxes can be bigger than a month of retail.
- **Content:** 1 reel a day. Making a coconut cup, packing an order, the wrap, "5 plastic-free Diwali gifts under ₹1,000", artisan intros.
- **Micro-influencers:** send hampers to 10–15 creators with 5k–50k followers (Kerala, lifestyle, sustainability, home). Ask for honest reels, not scripts.
- **Ads (small test):** Meta ads at ₹500–1,000/day on the hampers. Target metro cities + Malayalis outside Kerala + retarget site visitors.

### Week 2 (26 Oct–1 Nov): Last-order rush
- "Last day for Diwali delivery: 1 Nov" countdown on site, WhatsApp, Instagram.
- Re-send the WELCOMEBACK reminder to past customers who haven't ordered.
- Scale up ads that are working (cost per purchase below ~30% of order value); turn off the rest.

### Weeks 3–4 (2–15 Nov): After Diwali
- Ask every customer for a review (photo reviews get a small coupon).
- Switch the homepage to **Christmas & New Year gifting** (big in Kerala). Black Friday is 27 Nov.
- Start Google Shopping/Performance Max once you have ~20–30 purchases tracked.
- Write 4 helpful blog posts that people search for, e.g. "plastic-free Diwali gift ideas", "how to use a coconut shell cup", "virgin vs regular coconut oil", "corporate eco gifts India".

### Later (phase 2)
- Marketplaces for reach: Amazon (look at the **Amazon Karigar** programme for artisans) and Flipkart.
- International Malayali customers (Gulf, UK, US) once domestic operations are smooth.
- A subscription box (e.g. monthly "Kerala Pantry").
- Year-round calendar: Christmas, New Year, Pongal/Sankranti, Valentine's, Vishu, Earth Day (22 Apr), World Environment Day (5 Jun), Plastic Free July, Onam.

## 8. Numbers to watch

Track these weekly in Shopify Analytics:

| Metric | Suggested target |
|---|---|
| Conversion rate (orders ÷ visitors) | 1.5–2.5% |
| Average order value | ₹1,200+ |
| COD share / RTO (returned COD orders) | RTO below 15% |
| Repeat customer rate (90 days) | 15%+ |
| Cost per purchase (ads) | Below 30% of order value |

**Reality check:** at a 2% conversion rate, 100 orders a month needs about 5,000 visitors a month. So every week, ask: *where will next week's visitors come from?* Corporate orders are the fastest way to grow revenue without that much traffic.

## 10. Risks and backups

| Risk | Backup plan |
|---|---|
| Payment gateway KYC not approved by launch | Launch with COD only, keep KYC moving; or try a second gateway (Cashfree/PayU) in parallel |
| Email stops working after DNS change | Only change A/CNAME; screenshot all DNS records before touching anything |
| Hamper items run out | Build hampers from items you can restock within 3 days; mark "made to order, ships in 3 days" if needed |
| High COD returns | WhatsApp COD confirmation, prepaid discount, block repeat-RTO pincodes |
| Greenwashing complaints | Only specific, provable claims (section 3.4) |
| Brand confusion with other "EcoCart"s | Always use "EcoCart Kerala" in titles, ads and social handles |
